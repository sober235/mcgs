## 4. Pitfalls and do-not-redo
- `data/meas_*.h5` (2.4 GB each) are not in git and the generator refuses to overwrite them; the 1 sigma0 generator's
  nested check reads the 2 sigma0 files, so delete neither topic's `data/`.
- Card 5 carries another user's full-load job: heavy jobs there run 5 to 7 times slower (`--light_only_cards 5`).
- Other sessions' schedulers do not all honour `.gpulock`; the placeholder CUDA context in `run_all_noise.py` covers
  the data-loading gap of our jobs. Kill a scheduler, never its jobs, when restarting; restarting is safe (done jobs are
  skipped, running ones are waited for).
- `compare_noise_levels.py` reads all three topics; run it only after both noisy queues and finalizes are done.
- In the tool shell (zsh) unquoted `$VAR` does not split and `=====` is an expansion: put multi-argument commands in bash
  scripts.
- The `nested_vs_2x` check needs the 2 sigma0 draws to be the same realization: never regenerate one level alone.
- A 600-dpi PDF render holds about 140 GB of RAM; at most two at a time.

## 5. Why
- Ground truth and scale on the clean data, so the zero-noise topic is the exact zero-noise column (the gauge check
  shows it).
- Noise only on acquired samples: FLAIR's vendor shutter is outside the acquisition, and the known-zero detector needs it.
- One realization shared by both levels (common random numbers), so the two levels differ only in the noise scale.
- MC-GS frozen, classical lambdas re-selected: the question was the robustness of the recipe as it stands; the
  frozen-lambda view (record section 3.6) bounds what re-tuning is worth to the classical arms.
