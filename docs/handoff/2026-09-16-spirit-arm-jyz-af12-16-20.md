## 4. Pitfalls and do-not-redo

- zsh does not word-split unquoted `$var`: `set -- $spec`, `for x in $list`, `rsync $EXCLUDES` all pass ONE argument, and a glob with no
  match aborts the whole command. It bit four times in this work. Use bash scripts, arrays, or `--exclude-from`.
- `python … | head -1` in a render loop: harmless here but hides output (block-buffered) and can SIGPIPE the writer. Log to a file.
- `archive_and_relabel_figs.sh` first pass built `headerr_${tag}` with a doubled underscore, matched nothing, and the re-render overwrote
  the originals before they were moved; recovered from the committed worktree snapshots (PNG) and the topics' `export/` bundles (PDF),
  verification then run against the recovered files. Script fixed; check `mv` actually moved something before rendering.
- AF 12's inset figure of record comes from `scripts/fig_three_planes_inset.py`, not `fig_three_planes_compact.py` (AF 16 / 20 merged
  the inset layout into compact). AF 12 / 16 scripts hardcoded MC-INR; `--no_mcinr` added to reproduce the nine-method figures.
- The per-pixel 26 x 26 operator as an einsum over a (B,26,26,256,176) tensor re-permutes 6 GB per iteration and ran ~50x slower than the
  contiguous `[pixel, c, c']` matmul (`normal_matrix` / `applyP`); the two agree to 6e-7.
- `ptwt` in GSTMI lacks `periodization`; the DWT is written by hand (db2, 4 levels, periodised); the joint soft threshold commutes with
  the signed permutation between it and Wavelab's `FWT2_PO`, which is why the POCS output still matches to 1e-12.
- The single-precision port differs from MATLAB by ~1e-4 in the kernel (ill-conditioned normal equations); pass `--double` to
  `spirit_align_check.py` to see the 1e-12 agreement.
- `Results_20260908` is not under git; only the mirrors are.
