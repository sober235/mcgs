- `vdPoisMex` is time-seeded: never re-run `prep_02_mask.m` into this topic's `data/` — it would silently replace the mask
  every result depends on.
- The score json lands before the ~750 MB volumes npz; register an arm only after the MC-GS process has exited
  (`BadZipFile` otherwise).
- Card choice dominated wall time: ZS-SSDU ran 10x slower next to another user's job than on an idle card, with identical
  losses. Check `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps` and prefer a card with no compute process.
- `pgrep -f` inside a monitor script matches the monitor itself; test process liveness by pid (`kill -0`).
- `setup_af20_jyz.sh` v1 deleted itself (it was in its own `rm` list); the committed version is fixed.
- The worktree directory is named `mcgs_af16_wt` but carries this branch; the path in every script is intentional.
- The 48-voxel box leads do not transfer between masks (selection effect); quote the whole-volume margin.

## 5. Why
- Freezing the MC-GS parameters and changing only the mask makes every AF16→AF20 difference attributable to the mask;
  re-sweeping the classical λ keeps the competitor at its best. The comparison is therefore conservative for MC-GS.
