## 1. What the user asked for, and the bar

Two tasks in one message: add MC-INR (the Niessen 2025 reproduction) to the stage-2 table, then open a
Karpathy-style autoresearch loop that makes MC-GS the best arm of that table.

Three decisions were taken before any run (2026-09-14):

- **The bar**: MC-GS first in all six cells — head PSNR, SSIM, HFEN × T1w, T2s — against every other
  arm, **GS-single counted as a comparison method**, judged in the primary region (head inside the
  vendor's central 80 partitions).
- **Budget**: run until the target is met or the levers are exhausted.
- **GPUs**: any card with no compute process and utilisation ≤ 10 %, at most two of this line's
  trainings at once; stage 2's "card 2 only" rule retired.

Whatever lever the joint arm gets, the single arm gets — otherwise "joint beats single" would be won
by an unequal setting.

**Correction made in place**: the first version of that section read MC-GS's 0.161 against
L1-ESPIRiT's 0.166 as evidence that MC-GS fits the data better. It is not. The saved volume is
`ifft3(mf·y + (1−mf)·k_model)` — hard data consistency — so the GS arms agree with the reference at
the sampled positions by construction and the 0.161 is coil-combination leakage.

## 4. Round 1 — the prior weights, and why one of them had to be split

Four runs, one knob each, joint arm only:

| run | change | T1w head PSNR | min_margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | P10 transferred | 37.855 | −0.9744 |
| `p3_wavT1_006` | T1w wavelet 0.12 → 0.06 | 37.991 | −0.8389 |
| `p3_wavT1_003` | T1w wavelet 0.12 → 0.03 | 37.855 | −0.9751 |
| `p3_geo_006` | edge-TV λ_geo 0.10 → 0.06 | 38.116 | −0.7139 |
| `p3_geo_003` | edge-TV λ_geo 0.10 → 0.03 | 38.251 | −0.5786 |

The wavelet weight has an interior optimum near 0.06. The geometry weight does not: T1w improves to
the edge of the grid while T2s degrades monotonically past its bars — the long-TE, low-flip contrast
wants the smoothing the short-TE one does not.

So the geometry weight was given a strength per contrast, `lambda_geo_plain` /
`lambda_geo_contrast`, mirroring `lambda_wav_plain` / `lambda_wav_contrast`
(`paired_gs/losses/edge_tv.py::geo_term`, `tests/test_geo_term.py`, 4 tests). Both default to
`lambda_geo`, so the equal-weight path is the old arithmetic and earlier runs are bit-identical; 225
tests pass and a split-weight smoke prints `[geo] lambda_geo=0.1 (plain 0.02, contrast 0.1)`.

## 6. What the tuned single arm did to the question

`p3_single_T1w_c1` — the single-contrast arm at the same working point (T2s's own weights were not
changed by the tuning, so `gs_single_ar` is already its tuned single and only T1w's was re-run):

| T1w head | tuned single | tuned joint | joint − single |
|---|---|---|---|
| PSNR | **38.848** | 38.516 | **−0.332** (was −0.230 at P10) |
| SSIM | **0.9681** | 0.9668 | −0.0013 |
| HFEN | 0.0761 | 0.0774 | +0.0013 |

Two consequences:

1. **The best T1w reconstruction in this table is now a GS arm.** GS-single (tuned) passes
   L1-ESPIRiT on head PSNR (38.848 vs 38.830) and SSIM (0.9681 vs 0.9674); L1-ESPIRiT keeps head HFEN
   (0.0756 vs 0.0761). Tuning moved the single arm 0.762 dB.
2. **The joint deficit is structural and grows as the prior gets lighter** — −0.230 → −0.332. It is
   not a tuning artefact, which is what stage 2 could not rule out.

The cross-contrast coupling makes the trade explicit. `p3_edge0010` (common edge λ 0.001 on top of
c1) puts T2s above all three of its bars (34.197 / 0.9054 / 0.1837) and costs T1w 0.133 dB. On this
pair the short-TE contrast is the donor and the long-TE one the beneficiary; the target needs both.

Later levers, all at the c1 point: **6000 iterations** +0.072 dB on T1w (38.588, min_margin −0.260);
**6.0M points +0.275 dB** — T1w 38.791 / 0.9680 / 0.0770 and T2s 34.195, which is the first candidate
to put a cell **above** its bar (T2s head PSNR, +0.020) and leaves five margins at −0.0566 (T1w PSNR),
−0.0001, −0.0014, −0.0004, −0.0008. Capacity is the second big lever of this campaign, not the dead
end the jiangyizhou line saw.

## 6b. Joint against single at matched settings (the table that answers the question)

Every lever the joint arm got, the single arm got. T1w head PSNR in the primary region:

| matched setting | joint | single | joint − single |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4000 iterations, 3.6M points | 38.516 | 38.848 | **−0.332** |
| 6000 iterations, 3.6M points | 38.588 | 38.889 | **−0.301** |
| 4000 iterations, 6.0M points | 38.791 | 38.989 | **−0.198** |
| 4000 iterations, 9.6M points | 38.936 | 38.993 | **−0.057** |

The iteration lever is worth +0.072 to the joint arm and +0.041 to the single one — it closes 0.031 of
the gap. **Capacity is different: +0.275 to the joint arm against +0.141 to the single one, and the
gap closes by 0.134.** The joint arm gains twice what the single one does from the same points.

That has a mechanism. The joint arm carries **one** Gaussian cloud for **two** contrasts, so the
shared geometry is the first thing to starve; adding points relieves the joint arm specifically. Part
of what stage 2 read as "the shared-geometry prior is a net cost on this pair" is therefore capacity
starvation of the shared representation, not the prior itself — and it shrinks as the cloud grows:
−0.332 at 3.6M, −0.198 at 6.0M, both at 4000 iterations.

**The deficit is capacity starvation, and the two arms converge.** The single arm saturates after
6.0M points — 38.848, 38.989, 38.993 — while the joint keeps climbing — 38.516, 38.791, 38.936. The
gap therefore closes monotonically, −0.332, −0.198, −0.057, not because anything was tuned but
because the single hit its ceiling first and the joint had not. Read against that series, stage 2's
"joint is not better than single on this pair" and this stage's −0.332 are statements about a 3.6M
cloud, not about the contrast pair: one cloud carrying two contrasts needs more points than one
carrying one, which is the least surprising thing a shared representation could do, and neither the
earlier record nor the earlier line had tested it. Round 6 (19.2M, joint, queued 02:24) asks whether
the last 0.057 goes too; the single's matched point will follow before any claim is made.

Round 5 took both arms to 9.6M points. The joint arm keeps climbing on T1w — 38.516, 38.791, 38.936
for 3.6M, 6.0M, 9.6M, gains of +0.275 then +0.145, decelerating but real — and its matched single is
still running.

**But 9.6M turns T2s around.** T2s goes 34.163 / 0.9047 / 0.1857 at 3.6M to 34.176 / 0.9037 / 0.1871
at 9.6M: PSNR up 0.013, SSIM down 0.0010, HFEN up 0.0014, both now below the GS-single bar. Extra
Gaussians fit noise in the contrast that is SNR-limited rather than representation-limited, which is
the same asymmetry as the +0.275 / +0.032 split at 6.0M, now with a sign flip.

That is a structural obstacle, not a tuning one: **the joint arm has one cloud for two contrasts, so
capacity cannot be given per contrast** the way the wavelet and geometry weights now can. Every point
that buys T1w detail also lets T2s fit noise. The mechanism that starves the shared geometry is the
same one that forbids the obvious fix.

Until the matched single at 9.6M lands, §6's deficit should be quoted as "at 3.6M points", not as a
property of the contrast pair.

## 6c. Where both arms saturate (rounds 6–7)

Capacity runs out for the joint arm too. 19.2M points with the union guide give T1w 38.924 against
38.936 at 9.6M — flat, slightly down — while the matched single had already saturated at 38.993
(38.989 at 6.0M). **At their own saturation points the joint arm is about 0.07 dB behind the single
on T1w**: the deficit went 0.332 → 0.198 → 0.057 with capacity and then stopped closing.

The union guide (claude-eb's `loss.edge_tv_union_guide`, commit a22073f, each T2s edge-TV weight
becomes the minimum of its own and the plain render's) is what keeps T2s alive at high capacity. It
was verified here by scoring that session's `p4_union` through this topic's own `score_candidate`:
at 3.6M, T2s 34.1951 / 0.9054 / 0.1842 — all three above their bars — with T1w identical to c1 to the
fourth decimal. It matters more than the common-edge term because it is **joint-only**: it bounds one
contrast's smoothing by the other contrast's edges, and a single-contrast arm has no second contrast
to borrow from. Capacity, by contrast, is available to both arms, so it can close the gap but never
open one in the joint arm's favour.

At 19.2M + guide the six cells stand at +0.076 (T1w PSNR), +0.0003 (T1w SSIM), **−0.0012** (T1w HFEN,
against L1-ESPIRiT), +0.028 (T2s PSNR), **−0.0010** (T2s SSIM), +0.0001 (T2s HFEN) — four above,
two short by about a thousandth, against the *registered* bars. Against the matched single at its
saturation the T1w PSNR margin is −0.069, not +0.076. Round 7 (`p3_n96_union`, launched 07:25) takes
the capacity back to 9.6M where the joint arm's T1w peaked, keeping the guide.

## 7. How it ended (2026-09-15)

Final run `p3_n96_union_k05`: round-1c working point, 9.6M points, 4000 iterations, union guide on
with kappa scale 0.5. Primary region, T1w 38.9361 / 0.9685 / 0.0768, T2s 34.2273 / 0.9056 / 0.1836.

Against the **registered** bars that is five of six cells, T1w HFEN short by 0.0011. Against the
**honest** bars — the matched single at its own saturation, 38.9928 / 0.9687 / 0.0764 — the joint arm
leads all three T2s cells and trails all three T1w ones, by 0.057 dB, 0.0002 and 0.0012. **The
six-cell target was not met.**

It was stopped there rather than chased. T1w HFEN has no lever that does not cost T1w PSNR, and PSNR
is already 0.057 behind the matched single; grinding a third decimal on one subject with no held-out
exam is fitting the test set, and those digits would not survive a second subject.

What the campaign actually established, in order of what it is worth:

1. **The joint arm is the best T2s reconstruction in the table** — 34.227 / 0.9056 / 0.1836, all three
   head cells ahead of GS-single — and the mechanism that does it, the union guide, is the only one
   found on this line that a single-contrast arm cannot also have.
2. **Most of the stage-2 deficit was capacity starvation of the shared cloud.** −0.332 at 3.6M,
   −0.198 at 6.0M, −0.057 at 9.6M, with the single saturating after 6.0M and the joint still
   climbing. Every earlier comparison on this line, and the jiangyizhou line's capacity check too,
   ran at 3.6M.
3. **A residual 0.06 dB on T1w survives saturation.** That part is real, and it is the honest form of
   "the joint arm does not lead this exam".
4. **Two contrasts, opposite demands, one cloud.** T1w is representation-limited, T2s is SNR-limited,
   and capacity is the only knob that cannot be given per contrast.

Also worth keeping: the campaign corrected itself twice in the record rather than quietly — the
hard-data-consistency misreading in §3 and the capacity misreading in §6 — and both corrections came
from checking a surprising number against the ledger and the run's own json instead of the
notification that reported it.

## 8. Caveats that must travel with these numbers

- One subject, tuned on itself; this line has no held-out subject.
- The classical arms were λ-swept on the same subject, so MC-GS tuned here is comparable **with them**;
  ZS-SSDU, 3D-DIP, 3D-INR and MC-INR were not tuned and the tuned rows are not a fair comparison
  against those four.
- The reference is GRAPPA-completed 2× data, not a fully sampled acquisition.
- `grain_ratio` on T2s rests on 664 white-matter voxels and is not quoted.
- `p3_ceiling` is void: giving the trainer every acquirable position returns the reference itself
  (head PSNR 150.3, SSIM 1.0) because of the hard data consistency in §3. `--save_render` now writes
  the pure render (`recT1_render` / `recFL_render`) for whenever a capacity ceiling is wanted.
