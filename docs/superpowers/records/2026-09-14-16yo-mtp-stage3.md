## 1. What the user asked for, and the bar

Two tasks in one message: add MC-INR (the Niessen 2025 reproduction) to the stage-2 table, then open a
Karpathy-style autoresearch loop that makes MC-GS the best arm of that table.

Three decisions were taken before any run (2026-09-14):

- **The bar**: MC-GS first in all six cells — head PSNR, SSIM, HFEN × T1w, T2s — against every other
  arm, **GS-single counted as a comparison method**, judged in the primary region (head inside the
  vendor's central 80 partitions).
- **Budget**: run until the target is met or the levers are exhausted.
- **GPUs**: any card with no compute process and utilisation ≤ 10 %, at most two of this line's
  trainings at once; stage 2's "card 2 only" rule retired.

Whatever lever the joint arm gets, the single arm gets — otherwise "joint beats single" would be won
by an unequal setting.

## 2. The MC-INR arm (task one, finished)

## 3. Round 0 — where the T1w gap actually is

`scripts/diag_kspace_error.py` splits each arm's error energy over the phase-encode plane
(`logs/diag_kspace_error.log`). Energies relative to the GT's, ×1e3, T1w:

| | total | sampled | unsampled, in support | measured zeros | radius 0.25–0.50 | radius 0.50–1.00 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MC-GS | 8.74 | 0.161 | 8.542 | 0.039 | 1.513 | 6.877 |
| L1-ESPIRiT | 7.93 | 0.166 | 7.724 | 0.041 | 1.381 | 6.169 |

Of the 0.81 gap, 0.71 is in the 0.50–1.00 radius band and 0.13 in 0.25–0.50; below 0.25 MC-GS leads.
The sampled positions carry 1.8 % of the error and the measured zeros 0.45 %, which bounds any lever
that only reweights the fit to sampled data at ≈ 0.08 dB — `kspace_high_weight` was never run.

**Correction made in place**: the first version of that section read MC-GS's 0.161 against
L1-ESPIRiT's 0.166 as evidence that MC-GS fits the data better. It is not. The saved volume is
`ifft3(mf·y + (1−mf)·k_model)` — hard data consistency — so the GS arms agree with the reference at
the sampled positions by construction and the 0.161 is coil-combination leakage.

## 4. Round 1 — the prior weights, and why one of them had to be split

Four runs, one knob each, joint arm only:

| run | change | T1w head PSNR | min_margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | P10 transferred | 37.855 | −0.9744 |
| `p3_wavT1_006` | T1w wavelet 0.12 → 0.06 | 37.991 | −0.8389 |
| `p3_wavT1_003` | T1w wavelet 0.12 → 0.03 | 37.855 | −0.9751 |
| `p3_geo_006` | edge-TV λ_geo 0.10 → 0.06 | 38.116 | −0.7139 |
| `p3_geo_003` | edge-TV λ_geo 0.10 → 0.03 | 38.251 | −0.5786 |

The wavelet weight has an interior optimum near 0.06. The geometry weight does not: T1w improves to
the edge of the grid while T2s degrades monotonically past its bars — the long-TE, low-flip contrast
wants the smoothing the short-TE one does not.

So the geometry weight was given a strength per contrast, `lambda_geo_plain` /
`lambda_geo_contrast`, mirroring `lambda_wav_plain` / `lambda_wav_contrast`
(`paired_gs/losses/edge_tv.py::geo_term`, `tests/test_geo_term.py`, 4 tests). Both default to
`lambda_geo`, so the equal-weight path is the old arithmetic and earlier runs are bit-identical; 225
tests pass and a split-weight smoke prints `[geo] lambda_geo=0.1 (plain 0.02, contrast 0.1)`.

## 5. Round 1c — the working point

`p3_c1_wav006_gp003`: wavelet T1w 0.06 / T2s 0.10, geometry **T1w 0.03 / T2s 0.10**.

| head cell | baseline | shared 0.03 | c1 split |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1w PSNR | 37.855 | 38.251 | **38.516** |
| T1w SSIM | 0.9618 | 0.9660 | **0.9668** |
| T1w HFEN | 0.0922 | 0.0813 | **0.0774** |
| T2s PSNR | 34.160 | 33.927 | **34.163** |
| T2s SSIM | 0.9047 | 0.9028 | **0.9047** |
| T2s HFEN | 0.1858 | 0.1894 | **0.1857** |

**+0.661 dB on T1w against the +0.53 the two knobs gave separately, and no T2s cost at all.** Letting
the noisier contrast keep its smoothing evidently also cleans the geometry the two channels share,
which is the one mechanism a joint reconstruction has. `p3_c2_wav006_gp0015` (T1w geometry 0.015) is
identical to three decimals, so the knob is saturated below 0.03.

## 6. What the tuned single arm did to the question

`p3_single_T1w_c1` — the single-contrast arm at the same working point (T2s's own weights were not
changed by the tuning, so `gs_single_ar` is already its tuned single and only T1w's was re-run):

| T1w head | tuned single | tuned joint | joint − single |
|---|---|---|---|
| PSNR | **38.848** | 38.516 | **−0.332** (was −0.230 at P10) |
| SSIM | **0.9681** | 0.9668 | −0.0013 |
| HFEN | 0.0761 | 0.0774 | +0.0013 |

Two consequences:

1. **The best T1w reconstruction in this table is now a GS arm.** GS-single (tuned) passes
   L1-ESPIRiT on head PSNR (38.848 vs 38.830) and SSIM (0.9681 vs 0.9674); L1-ESPIRiT keeps head HFEN
   (0.0756 vs 0.0761). Tuning moved the single arm 0.762 dB.
2. **The joint deficit is structural and grows as the prior gets lighter** — −0.230 → −0.332. It is
   not a tuning artefact, which is what stage 2 could not rule out.

The cross-contrast coupling makes the trade explicit. `p3_edge0010` (common edge λ 0.001 on top of
c1) puts T2s above all three of its bars (34.197 / 0.9054 / 0.1837) and costs T1w 0.133 dB. On this
pair the short-TE contrast is the donor and the long-TE one the beneficiary; the target needs both.

Later levers, all at the c1 point: **6000 iterations** +0.072 dB on T1w (38.588, min_margin −0.260);
**6.0M points +0.275 dB** — T1w 38.791 / 0.9680 / 0.0770 and T2s 34.195, which is the first candidate
to put a cell **above** its bar (T2s head PSNR, +0.020) and leaves five margins at −0.0566 (T1w PSNR),
−0.0001, −0.0014, −0.0004, −0.0008. Capacity is the second big lever of this campaign, not the dead
end the jiangyizhou line saw.

## 8. Caveats that must travel with these numbers

- One subject, tuned on itself; this line has no held-out subject.
- The classical arms were λ-swept on the same subject, so MC-GS tuned here is comparable **with them**;
  ZS-SSDU, 3D-DIP, 3D-INR and MC-INR were not tuned and the tuned rows are not a fair comparison
  against those four.
- The reference is GRAPPA-completed 2× data, not a fully sampled acquisition.
- `grain_ratio` on T2s rests on 664 white-matter voxels and is not quoted.
- `p3_ceiling` is void: giving the trainer every acquirable position returns the reference itself
  (head PSNR 150.3, SSIM 1.0) because of the hard data consistency in §3. `--save_render` now writes
  the pure render (`recT1_render` / `recFL_render`) for whenever a capacity ceiling is wanted.
