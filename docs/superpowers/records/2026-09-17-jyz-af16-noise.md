## 0. Result

**The frozen AF16 phase-3 MC-GS recipe is not robust to 2 sigma0 of added measurement noise.** Its head PSNR falls
5.9 dB (T1) and 5.0 dB (FLAIR), and from first on all six head cells it drops to 8th / 7th / 5th (T1: PSNR / SSIM /
HFEN) and 9th / 9th / 7th (FLAIR) of twelve arms. **At 1 sigma0 it keeps head PSNR and head HFEN and loses head SSIM**:
first on FLAIR head PSNR (+0.007 dB over GS-single), second on T1 head PSNR (0.003 dB behind the converged TV), first
on head HFEN in both contrasts, fifth on head SSIM in both. The GS render takes the noise in (sharpness ratio of MC-GS
0.86 / 0.80 at zero noise, 0.96 / 0.90 at 1 sigma0, 1.33 / 1.23 at 2 sigma0), while every classical family re-selects
a 1.4 to 4 times larger lambda against the ground truth; hard data consistency accounts for at most 0.06 dB.

## 2. Design: one variable, the measurements

Held fixed: subject vol3_jiangyizhou (CFL, 26 coils, 256 x 256 x 176), the ESPIRiT maps, the house vd-Poisson disc mask
`T1FLAIR_AF16_v3_jiangyizhou/data/mask_af16_3d.h5` (2966 PE points, md5 d08179b8e74f35846455983dbe299294; T1 15.19x,
2733 of the points carry FLAIR data), the ground truth and scale from the clean k-space, the scorer
`t1t2_common.Pair.score`, every arm's settings, and the MC-GS recipe (the recipe block of `run_noise_mcgs.sh` is
byte-identical to `run_diffmask_mcgs.sh`).

**Noise.** n ~ CN(0, sigma^2), independent over coils and samples, added only on the acquired samples (T1 the whole PE
plane; FLAIR the 34139 of 45056 lines inside the vendor shutter, outside stays exactly zero).

```
sigma      = k * sigma0_k            k = 1 or 2
sigma0_k   = sigma0_img / sqrt(f)    f = acquired fraction of the PE plane
sigma0_img = air noise of the clean ground truth (gauge units, P99.9 = 1)

T1     sigma0_img 0.01408   f 1.0000   sigma0_k 0.01408
FLAIR  sigma0_img 0.01282   f 0.7577   sigma0_k 0.01472
```

One realization: numpy PCG64 from `SeedSequence(20260916)`, one child per contrast (T1 first). The 1 sigma0 noise is the
2 sigma0 noise halved (common random numbers), so the two levels differ only in the noise scale.

**Generator checks** (`logs/make_noisy_kspace.log` of each topic; all passed at both levels): zero outside the support;
per-coil mean within 4 standard errors (52 tests); per-coil standard deviation within 0.5 %; air noise of the noisy full
image within 2 % of sqrt(sigma0_img^2 + sigma^2 f) (2 sigma0: T1 0.03148 expected / 0.03147 measured, FLAIR 0.02866 /
0.02865; 1 sigma0: 0.01991 / 0.01990 and 0.01812 / 0.01812); ground truth within 1e-4 and scale within 1e-5 of the AF16
topic; at 1 sigma0 also 2 (meas_1x - clean) = meas_2x - clean within single-precision rounding (largest ratio 0.29 /
0.29, limit 1).

## 4. Reading

- **2 sigma0: not robust.** MC-GS loses 5.9 / 5.0 dB head PSNR; the margin over the strongest comparison arm goes from
  +0.148 / +0.042 to -3.328 (ZS-SSDU) / -2.606 (JTV); head SSIM and HFEN follow (-0.1180 / -0.1165 and -0.0382 /
  -0.0359). ZS-SSDU becomes the best T1 arm on head PSNR, JTV the best FLAIR arm.
- **1 sigma0: PSNR and HFEN hold, SSIM does not.** Margins +0.007 (FLAIR PSNR), -0.003 (T1 PSNR), +0.0053 / +0.0004
  (HFEN), -0.0066 / -0.0061 (SSIM, both against the converged TV). The T1 PSNR order rests on 0.003 dB; the recipe's
  seed-to-seed spread on clean data is 0.001 dB and was not measured on noisy data.
- **Mechanism.** The GS render fits the noise: the sharpness ratio of MC-GS rises 0.855 -> 0.958 -> 1.331 (T1) and
  0.804 -> 0.899 -> 1.227 (FLAIR), GS-single's alike. Doubling the noise multiplies the MC-GS head-PSNR loss by 3.5 /
  3.9 and the converged TV's by 2.4 / 2.4. Hard data consistency is not the cause: without it the same volumes gain
  0.04 / 0.02 dB (1 sigma0) and 0.06 / 0.06 dB (2 sigma0).
- **What the frozen recipe pays.** The classical families move their lambda up with the noise (TV 0.0007 -> 0.001 ->
  0.002 on T1, 0.0005 -> 0.001 -> 0.002 on FLAIR; JTV 0.001 / 0.0007 -> 0.0014 -> 0.0028; L1-SPIRiT 0.001 -> 0.002 ->
  0.004), worth 0.1 to 0.4 dB at 1 sigma0 and 1.1 to 1.9 dB at 2 sigma0 over their zero-noise lambda (section 3.6). With
  those families frozen as well, MC-GS would lead T1 head PSNR at 1 sigma0 by +0.066 dB (over ZS-SSDU, which has no
  lambda) and FLAIR by +0.007 (GS-single), would still trail ZS-SSDU on head SSIM (-0.0058 / -0.0037), and at 2 sigma0
  would still trail ZS-SSDU by 3.3 / 2.4 dB. This is read off sections 3.1, 3.2 and 3.6; no extra run.
- **Arms without an explicit regulariser** lose the most (MC-INR -1.9 / -1.5 dB at 1 sigma0, -6.6 / -5.7 at 2 sigma0);
  3D-DIP, early-stopped on held-out k-space, loses almost nothing (at most 0.05 dB) but starts 4.3 to 4.7 dB below
  MC-GS;
  ZS-SSDU loses 0.2 / 0.7 and 1.0 / 1.7 dB, the smallest loss among the strong arms.
- **Coupling.** MC-GS minus GS-single (head PSNR) grows on T1 (+0.148 -> +0.217 -> +0.232) and shrinks to a loss on
  FLAIR (+0.042 -> +0.007 -> -0.116).

## 6. How it was run

- Scheduler `scripts/run_all_noise.py` per topic (one job of this user per card, the shared `.gpulock.<card>` lock
  directories, the working directory in the job fingerprint, one retry), launched with `scripts/launch_noise.sh`
  (`setsid nohup`), finalize `scripts/finalize_noise.sh` (register the GS arms, MC-INR scoring, the render diagnostic,
  `summarize.py`, README tables, `compare_noise.py`, figures).
- 2 sigma0: armed 09-16 21:48 on card 5, 21:50 on cards 1, 2, 5; queue empty 09-17 05:23, finalize 05:56; grid
  extension 05:57 to 07:30, second finalize 08:03. 1 sigma0: armed 09-16 23:37 on cards 2, 1, 5; queue empty 09-17
  08:56, finalize about 09:30. No failures at either level.
- Two schedulers shared cards 1, 2, 5. The 2 sigma0 queue took a freed card first in each round, so the levels ran
  mostly one after the other.
- 00:30: the CE retrospective study's scheduler (another session) put an INR job on card 1 while one of ours was still
  loading data on the CPU; that session withdrew its job and now excludes 1, 2, 5 and honours the lock directories. Both
  of our schedulers gained a placeholder CUDA context on the card of every job from launch until the job is reaped
  (`HOLD_S=240`) and were restarted at 01:22 (schedulers only; running jobs untouched).
- 03:25: card 5 carries another user's full-load job; a 3D-INR job ran there at 17 s/it (2.3 s/it on a free card).
  Both schedulers gained `--light_only_cards 5` (no GS, INR, DIP, ZS-SSDU or MC-INR on card 5); the slow job was killed
  and rerun on card 2 (59.5 min).
- Scale check after the queues: every per-contrast job file records the AF16 topic's `scale_p999` (26 files at 1 sigma0,
  28 at 2 sigma0, worst relative difference 0); `summary.json` has 24 rows (12 arms x 2 contrasts) at both levels.

## 7. Caveats

- One subject and one realization; the spread over realizations and over subjects is not measured.
- The added noise is white and independent across coils; the scanner's own noise is correlated across coils. The level
  is defined on the combined image, so the ratio to sigma0 holds on average.
- Maps and ground truth are clean; only L1-SPIRiT's kernel sees the noisy calibration block. The ground truth carries
  the scanner's own noise sigma0.
- The classical lambdas are chosen against the ground truth while MC-GS runs a frozen recipe; this favours the classical
  arms (section 3.6 bounds the effect).
- The GS arms are scored with hard data consistency, which returns the noise on the acquired points to the image;
  section 3.7 shows it costs at most 0.06 dB.
- The zero-noise JTV sweep had two points (section 3.5).
- The T1 head-PSNR order at 1 sigma0 rests on 0.003 dB.
