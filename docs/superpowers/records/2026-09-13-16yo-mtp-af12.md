## 1. What was decided and what was built

The user's decisions: AF 12 counted as sampled points over the whole phase-encode matrix with the ACS
included; the known-zero region given to **every** method, not only to the GS arms as in the earlier
topics; the 23 GB of unwanted stage-1 contrast outputs left alone; all nine arms. Three further choices
were left to the design and taken as its defaults: the budget follows the user's own AF wording (2635
points, not 1660), the primary table is the head inside the vendor's central 80 partitions, and the line
keeps its GPU-2-only rule.

Built: a support-restricted vd-Poisson mask generator (`uih_mtp/retro_mask.py`), a region-restricted
rescorer on the frozen gauge (`uih_mtp/rescore.py`), the topic's arm scripts retargeted from the AF16
jiangyizhou topic, and 26 queued jobs, all of which returned 0.

MC-GS does not lead this exam, and joint does not beat single on it.

L1-ESPIRiT is first on every T1w column (PSNR, SSIM, HFEN, NMSE); on T2s the two GS arms are first on
everything except sharpness. MC-GS minus GS-single is −0.231 dB on T1w and −0.015 dB on T2s, with SSIM,
HFEN and sharpness level or worse on both.

**The classical joint arm agrees.** Joint SENSE minus the converged independent CS-SENSE is −0.90 dB on
T1w and +0.06 dB on T2s: the same pattern, from an implementation that shares no code with MC-GS. Two
independent shared-geometry methods failing the same way indicts the contrast pair, not the code. These
are the two least similar of the ten acquired contrasts (brain-mask r = 0.57) — a short-TE T1-weighted
image against a long-TE T2*-weighted one whose susceptibility foci, the basis of the calcified-nodule
appearance, are by construction not edges of the T1w image.

This contradicts the adult finding. On the AF16 T1 + FLAIR pairs, joint minus single was positive in all
four channels, and that study's record called the growth of the joint advantage with sparser data its one
qualitative new fact. Here the data are sparser still (AF 12 of the matrix, 7.56x of the acquired
support) and the advantage is absent. The boundary condition is the similarity of the pair, not the
sparsity.

## 3. Mask and protocol

2635 of 31616 (PE 304 x SPE 104) positions, AF 12.00 of the matrix and 7.56x of the 19914 acquired ones,
one mask for both contrasts. The house generator draws on a full rectangle, so the mask is built by
selection and trimming: 105 isotropic draws (`a` 2.6 .. 3.6), take the draw whose in-support count first
reaches the budget (`draw_052`, a = 3.1, 2664 inside), trim the outermost 29 points. Z = 11702 positions
(partial Fourier PE 227..303 and the elliptical corners) is measured zero and enters every arm's forward
operator; self-supervised splits are drawn from the sampling mask alone.

Every λ was swept on this subject and every scan ends with its optimum strictly inside its grid — three
scans had to be extended to get there (CS-SENSE T1w down to 0.00025, Joint SENSE bracketed both ways,
L1-ESPIRiT down to 0.00035 on T1w and 0.001 on T2s). CS-SENSE 11 values, L1-ESPIRiT 14, Joint SENSE 5.

## 4. Four corrections made during execution

1. **The house generator does not fully sample its calibration block.** The design's acceptance criterion
   asked for a fully sampled 24 x 24 square. `vdPoisMex`'s `calib` biases the density near DC instead: the
   adult topics' own masks sample 472 (AF16) and 482 (AF12) of their 576 calibration positions, full only
   out to 16 x 16. This plane's mask samples 366. Recorded, not enforced; harmless because every arm
   reconstructs with the ESPIRiT maps of the complete reference and none self-calibrates from the mask.
2. **The GS arms were getting no known zeros at all.** The smoke run reported `frac_z_used = 0.0000`. The
   `known_zero_support` config key was added to the framework by `e0e1058`, after `3a44aba`, the commit
   this worktree was cut from, and the fallback estimator finds nothing here because the sampling mask
   never lands outside the support. The framework half of that commit and its tests were applied here
   (same lineage, default path unchanged, 23 known-zero tests pass). Afterwards both channels report
   `frac_z_used = 0.3701` = 11702 / 31616, `nonzero_in_z = 0`, `kz_energy = 0`.
3. **The plan would have made the gauge check circular.** It said to rewrite `recon_classical.py` on top
   of the shared `t1t2_common`. But `check_gauge.py` exists to re-score one of that script's volumes
   *through* the shared module and show two independent transcriptions agree; had the script imported the
   module, the check would have compared the module with itself. The script kept its own transcription and
   only gained the second mask. `GAUGE OK`, nine metrics, differences 0.00e+00.
4. **The axis-to-plane mapping was inherited wrong.** The AF16 scripts label axis 0 transverse. Here the
   volume is (RO, PE, SPE) and SPE is the partition axis the vendor exports, so axis 2 is transverse,
   axis 0 coronal, axis 1 sagittal. Adjudicated against the vendor DICOM rather than assumed:
   `gt[:, 2:-2, 52]` equals vendor slice 39 with r = 0.899. Every figure script was corrected, and the
   panel aspect was fixed to the true 0.7 x 0.7 x 1.35 mm proportions (the two non-transverse planes were
   squashed by 1.93x).

## 5. Method notes worth keeping

- **Slice choice.** `best_slice_mcgs.py` ranks slices by how far MC-GS leads. MC-GS never leads on T1w, so
  that criterion would have picked the slice least unflattering to it. The figures use anatomy instead:
  transverse SPE 53 = vendor slice 38, the level where the stage-1 survey saw the subependymal nodules;
  coronal RO 160 and sagittal PE 152 are the head-area centroids.
- **The rescorer had to be parallelised.** The frozen 15³ LoG convolution is single-threaded and costs
  about 68 G multiply-adds per arm; serially the 18 volumes would have taken three hours per gauge. Scoring
  arms in parallel processes cut it to minutes. The scoring functions were not touched, and the full-volume
  run reproduces every arm's own numbers: 90 values across 18 arm/contrast pairs, all within 2e-4.
- **`grain_ratio` is unusable on T2s.** The frozen white-matter mask keeps 220039 voxels on T1w and 664 on
  T2s: the strict P55–P90 band plus a σ 2 erosion leaves almost nothing on a contrast whose tissue
  intensities are less spatially coherent. The gauge was not changed; the number is flagged in the README.
- **ZS-SSDU is cheap here** (early stop at epoch 43, 29 minutes, against 5 hours on the adult AF16 data)
  and lands mid-table. 3D-DIP and 3D-INR are 6–8 dB behind, as in every earlier topic.
- Timings on one card: MC-GS 65 min, each GS-single 65 min, classical arms 5 min each, ZS-SSDU 27 min,
  DIP 46 min, INR 52 min, L1-ESPIRiT on the CPU about 8 min per λ.
