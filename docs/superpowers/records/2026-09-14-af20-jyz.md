## 2. Design — one variable

Everything is the AF 16 topic except the mask: same subject, CFL k-space, ESPIRiT maps (symlinks into the AF 12 topic),
ground truth, gauge, arms and arm arguments. The MC-GS parameters are frozen at the AF 16 phase-3 recipe (P6 cold start,
3.6M points, edge-TV 0.10 / κ_q 0.85, kspace_high 2.2, db4 finest level T1 0.12 / FLAIR 0.10, common-edge 0.001,
phase TV 0.03, 4000 it); every recipe line of the AF 20 run log was checked against `p3_r3_e0001_ptv003_full4k`.
The classical λ are **re-swept** on the AF 20 mask, grids centred on the AF 16 optima and extended wherever an optimum
first landed on an edge; all six curves end with an interior optimum. ZS-SSDU / DIP / INR use the AF 16 topic's
arguments (epochs 100; 5000 it lr 3e-3; 1500 it lr 5e-3).

**Mask** `vdPoisMex(256,176,256,176, 5, 4, 24, 0, 1)`: 2427 of 45056 PE points (5.39 %), T1 18.56x, FLAIR 19.96x on the
plane and 15.13x against its own shutter, 24x24 centre 474 / 576 points. Two properties matter for reading the results:
- `vdPoisMex` seeds from `time(0)`: `prep_02_mask.m` cannot reproduce this mask; `data/mask_vdpois_256x176_af20.mat`
  (md5 1c90baf850a8c1e64b43f6c64146e2c9) is the only copy and `*.mat` is gitignored.
- The house masks are independent draws, not nested: 698 of the 2427 AF 20 points are in the AF 16 mask (AF 16 has 812
  of its 2966 in AF 12). AF 16 → AF 20 changes the sample positions as well as their number; the draw-to-draw variance
  of the margin on this data was never measured.

Price of the step from AF 16 to AF 20, head PSNR: MC-GS −0.874 / −0.749, GS-single −0.891 / −0.760, L1-ESPIRiT
−0.838 / −0.815, converged TV −0.942 / −0.807, JTV −0.947 / −0.811, CG-SENSE −0.929 / −0.737, **ZS-SSDU −1.258 /
−1.344**, 3D-INR −0.919 / −0.447, 3D-DIP +0.002 / −0.012.

- MC-GS is first on all six head cells; the whole-volume margin holds (mean 0.461 → 0.476, the smaller of the two
  rises 0.425 → 0.461). **It does not grow with the acceleration**: the idea of reaching the 1.0 dB target by raising
  the acceleration is not supported. The SSIM and HFEN leads do widen on both contrasts.
- **T1 sharpness is now below L1-ESPIRiT's** (the phase-3 sharpness gate, met at AF 16, fails on T1 at AF 20).
- The coupling term is worth about the same one acceleration up (+0.017 / +0.010 dB). GS-single alone is 0.30 / 0.44 dB
  above L1-ESPIRiT: most of the margin is the representation and its priors.
- ZS-SSDU loses the most of any arm (it early-stopped at epoch 53 / 41). 3D-DIP does not move because it is budget-
  limited at both accelerations (best held-out k-space residual at iteration 5000 at AF 16 and 4900 at AF 20, smooth
  output, sharpness 0.64): its error is under-fitting, not missing data.
- The comparison is conservative for MC-GS: every classical λ was re-swept at AF 20, the MC-GS parameters were not.
- An observation only (one subject, two points): FLAIR at AF 20 (15.13x on its shutter) has margin +0.491 and T1 at
  AF 16 (15.19x) had +0.497.

## 5. How it was run (wall times are not comparable with AF 16)

Cards were shared throughout. MC-GS joint ran at 4.6–5.9 s per iteration on card 6 next to another user's three-day-old
job (1.0 s/it at AF 16), 5.8 h for 4000 it. Stage 2 first queued behind the MC-GS runs on cards 5/6 and ran ZS-SSDU at
~530 s per epoch; when cards 0/2/3 went idle both ZS-SSDU runs were stopped (logs in `logs/stage2_aborted/`) and
`run_stage2_af20.sh` v2 re-placed one chain per idle card, each job starting only on a card with no compute process
(52 s for the same first epoch; identical losses to four digits). Everything finished at 00:42 on 2026-09-14.

  measured the prior at −0.013 dB.

  well below L1-ESPIRiT). It has not been run at AF 20, so the AF 20 table is nine arms, the AF 16 table ten.

## 8. Mask-draw variance of the margin (follow-up, 2026-09-14 09:00–11:45)

Three more draws of the same `vdPoisMex` call (b, c, d; `scripts/prep_02b_mask_draws.m`, `pause(2)` between calls
because the generator seeds from `time(0)`; the registered mask untouched, md5 unchanged). Per draw only the two arms

two `mask_path` lines only; the draw-b trainer's log reads `zero_lines=170/2410`) and L1-ESPIRiT at the AF 20 optimum and

- L1-ESPIRiT's best λ is the interior 0.001 (T1) / 0.003 (FLAIR) on every draw.
- **The margin is a paired quantity and far more stable than either arm.** Across draws the absolute head PSNR moves
  with SD 0.009 / 0.007 (T1, MC-GS / L1) and 0.040 / 0.040 (FLAIR), the two arms move together (correlation +0.97 /
  +0.99), and their difference has SD 0.002 / 0.004 — four to ten times smaller.
- **The AF 16 → AF 20 margin shifts are real on this evidence**: −0.038 dB on T1 and +0.063 dB on FLAIR against the
  four-draw means, 16 and 15 draw SDs. Two caveats bound this: an SD from four draws is itself uncertain (its upper 95 %
  limit is ~3.7x the estimate, which would still leave the shifts at 4–5 SD), and AF 16 had one draw, whose spread is
  assumed to match AF 20's.
- **The T1 sharpness gate fails on all four draws, and FLAIR's passes on all four**: the T1 sharpness result is a
  property of AF 20 with this recipe, not of one mask.
- Draw point counts vary (2400–2427, T1 AF 18.56–18.77) and the registered draw has the most points; both arms are
  highest on it, the margin is not.

**Incident, recorded because it cost a run and could have hung a card.** The first queue put MC-GS draws b, c and d
all on card 5: `card_idle()` piped `nvidia-smi` into `grep -q` under `set -o pipefail`, grep's early exit gave
nvidia-smi a SIGPIPE, the pipeline failed and the leading `!` reported the busy card as idle (reproduced: 5/5 checks
said idle for busy cards 5 and 6; only card 7, whose match is the last line, read busy). Draw d died of CUDA OOM next
to b and c and was logged `rc=0` because `$(date)` in the same echo ran before `$?` expanded; draw c was stopped after
seven minutes; b kept its card. Fixed in `run_mask_draws_af20.sh` and `run_stage2_af20.sh` (read the listing into a
variable, then grep; an empty bus id counts as busy; capture rc first). Draws c and d were rerun with
`requeue_mcgs_draws_af20.sh`, which confirms each trainer on its card before placing the next; when another session
took card 5 thirty seconds after draw b exited, draw d was handed off to card 3 the moment draw c's trainer exited
(`handoff_draw_d_af20.sh`). The `ALL_MASK_DRAWS_DONE` line at 10:20 in `logs/mask_draws_af20.log` is from the first
orchestrator and predates draws c and d.
