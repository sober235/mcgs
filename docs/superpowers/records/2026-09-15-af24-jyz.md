## 2. Design — one variable

Everything is the AF 20 topic except the mask: same subject, CFL k-space, ESPIRiT maps (symlinks into the AF 12 topic),
ground truth, gauge, arms and arm arguments. MC-GS is frozen at the AF 16 phase-3 recipe; the recipe block of
`run_af24_mcgs.sh` is byte-identical to `run_af20_mcgs.sh`. The configs differ from the AF 20 ones only in data paths,
`acceleration_factor` (read only by the trainer's threshold-10 strategy switch, which 16, 20 and 24 all pass) and the
experiment name. The classical λ are re-swept: grids = the AF 20 topic's final grids plus one point below each (the one
optimum that moved from AF 16 to AF 20 moved down). ZS-SSDU / DIP / INR use the AF 16 topic's arguments.

**Mask.** House generator and settings, `vdPoisMex(256,176,256,176, accelx, 4, 24, 0, 1)`; only accelx differs. A
draw's point count is random, so `scripts/prep_02_mask_pool_af24.m` drew 134 masks (stage A: accelx 6.4–7.0, two draws

`scripts/prep_03_pick_mask_af24.py` applied a rule fixed before the pool was drawn: the lowest-index draw with exactly
1877 points, untrimmed. Two draws qualified; **draw_025 (accelx 6.8656)** was taken (the other gives FLAIR 25.37x).

`scripts/run_all_af24.py`, a priority queue: one GPU job of this user per card (other sessions' jobs count), at most
four GPU jobs, twelve L1-ESPIRiT runs in four CPU slots, finalize when empty. A queue rather than fixed cards because at
21:40 five of the eight cards carried this user's other sessions' jobs; every job's argv signature was unit-checked to
match only itself. Smoke first (card 6, 60 it, warmup 20): `rc=0`, `actual acceleration: 24.00x`, numbers not evidence.
Launched 21:45:49, queue empty 05:22, finalize done 05:30; no failure, no retry. Wall time: MC-GS joint 454 min (card 6,
next to another user's job, ~6.8 s/it), GS-single FLAIR 457 min (card 7), GS-single T1 224 min (card 3); L1-ESPIRiT
10–12 min each; converged TV 5–12 min; joint TV 8 min; ZS-SSDU 42 / 47 min; INR 60 / 60 min; DIP 52 / 65 min. The first
four L1-ESPIRiT runs held their data on card 6 next to MC-GS joint (no classical job was running yet, so the rule fell
back to the card with most free memory; peak about 61 of 80 GB, no incident).

- Magnitude gauge only; the complex gauge was not examined at AF 24.
- Exactly 24x holds for T1 by construction; FLAIR is at 25.51x.

## 11. The acceleration label (user decision, 2026-09-16)

The user asked why FLAIR's number differed from T1's and what the real factor is. The raw CFL settles the fact: T1 acquired
all 45056 PE lines, FLAIR 34139 of them (10917 lines exactly zero over 256 readout samples x 26 coils: the corners outside the
inscribed ellipse, and ky rows 0-19). "Fully sampled" holds in the sense that neither scan was accelerated, but FLAIR's scan
never covered the whole plane. With the one shared mask that gives three numbers for AF 24 FLAIR: 24.00x by mask points,
25.51x by points carrying data (the 2026-09-14 per-contrast gauge), 19.33x against FLAIR's own acquisition.

**Decision: label both contrasts 24.00x** (plane points / sampled points), with the FLAIR figures stating under the panels
that 1766 of the 1877 sampled points carry FLAIR data. Re-rendered under this label: the `_p3_mcinr_spirit` figures of record
(PNG + PDF, also replaced inside the export bundle), the three mask figures, the raw-support figure, and the AF12_16_20_24
trend topic (its AF axis is now the mask gauge on both contrasts). Sections written before 2026-09-16 quote the per-contrast
gauge and are left as the record of what was written then. **AF 12 / 16 / 20 still carry the per-contrast FLAIR labels
(12.86 / 16.49 / 19.96); under this decision they become 11.71 / 15.19 / 18.56. The session that keeps those topics (claude-ad)
has closed, so that relabel is unowned — it is figure titles, README lines and the trend topic of that line, no GPU.**
