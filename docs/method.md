# Method — Paired dual-contrast 3D Gaussian Splatting MRI reconstruction

**Multi-contrast joint reconstruction: shared geometry, dual density.** One set of 3D
Gaussian geometry is constrained jointly by the k-space data-consistency terms of *two*
co-registered contrasts. This document is the code-verified derivation (`paired_gs/`:
`gaussian/joint_gaussian_model.py`, `gaussian/kspace_renderer.py`,
`gaussian/voxelizer.py`, `trainers/joint_trainer.py`, `data/transforms.py`), written in
the framework's contrast-agnostic vocabulary (**plain** = high-SNR guide channel,
**contrast** = guided channel). The shipped instance maps plain→T1, contrast→FLAIR.

---

## 1. Variables and observation model

For one subject, two contrasts $m\in\{p,c\}$: $p$ = **plain** (high-SNR guide),
$c$ = **contrast** (guided). The goal is to jointly reconstruct two complex 3D volumes
$x_p, x_c \in \mathbb{C}^{D\times H\times W}$ from two sets of multi-coil, 3D Cartesian
undersampled k-space. Per-scan self-supervised (no ground truth in training).

$$
y_m \;=\; M_m \odot \big(\mathcal{F}_{3c}\,(C_m \odot x_m)\big) \;+\; \varepsilon_m,
\qquad m\in\{p,c\}
$$

- $C_m\in\mathbb{C}^{N_{\text{coil}}\times D\times H\times W}$: coil sensitivity maps
  (CSM, fixed input `csm_p`/`csm_c`).
- $\mathcal{F}_{3c}$: orthonormal, centered 3D FFT (`fft3c` =
  `fftshift ∘ fftn(norm="ortho") ∘ ifftshift`, energy-preserving).
- $M_m\in\{0,1\}^{D\times H\times W}$: undersampling mask (`mask_p`/`mask_c`, broadcast over coils).
- $y_m$: multi-coil undersampled k-space (`kspace_p`/`kspace_c`).

Forward operator $A_m(\cdot)=M_m\odot\mathcal{F}_{3c}(C_m\odot\,\cdot\,)$, i.e.
$y_m=A_m x_m+\varepsilon_m$.

## 2. Representation: shared geometry + dual-density Gaussian primitives

$N$ anisotropic 3D Gaussian primitives, **geometry shared across both contrasts**, only
the complex amplitude split per contrast:

$$
\Theta_{\text{shared}}=\{\mu_n,\ s_n,\ q_n\}_{n=1}^{N},
\qquad
\alpha_{n}^{(p)},\,\alpha_{n}^{(c)}\in\mathbb{C}.
$$

The spatial kernel of primitive $n$ (normalized coordinates $[-1,1]^3$):

$$
g_n(u)=\exp\!\Big(-\tfrac12\,(u-\mu_n)^\top \Sigma_n^{-1}(u-\mu_n)\Big),
\qquad
\Sigma_n = R(q_n)\,\mathrm{diag}(s_n^2)\,R(q_n)^\top,
$$

with $R(q_n)$ the rotation matrix of unit quaternion $q_n$. Both contrast images are
generated by the **same** $\{\mu_n,\Sigma_n\}$, differing only in per-primitive amplitude:

$$
\boxed{\,x_m(u)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\alpha_n^{(m)}\,g_n(u),\qquad m\in\{p,c\}\,}
$$

Code: `JointGaussianModel3D` extends `GaussianModel3D` (parent density = plain channel,
adds a parallel contrast channel). During densify/prune the contrast rows follow the
shared geometry (`_update_params`), so both channels always share one geometry.

> **Novelty carrier**: two data-consistency terms $A_p, A_c$ jointly constrain the same
> $\{\mu_n,\Sigma_n\}$ — turning "is this 3D primitive supported by the target contrast's
> measurement" into a quantity the undersampled data can directly adjudicate.

## 3. Rendering: two equivalent forward paths

**(a) Spatial voxelization** (primary in training, `Voxelizer.forward`) — each primitive
scatters over a $3\sigma$-truncated neighborhood by Mahalanobis distance:

$$
x_m[v]=\sum_{n:\,v\in\mathcal{N}_n}\alpha_n^{(m)}\exp\!\Big(-\tfrac12\,\delta_{vn}^\top\Sigma_n^{-1}\delta_{vn}\Big),
\quad
\mathcal{N}_n=\{v:\ \delta_{vn}^\top\Sigma_n^{-1}\delta_{vn}\le 9\}.
$$

Coordinate map $\mu_n^{\text{vox}}=(\mu_n+1)\tfrac12[D,H,W]-\tfrac12$; radius
$3\max_i s_{n,i}$ (clamped to `max_radius`).

**(b) Analytic k-space rendering** (`gaussian_kspace_transform`) — the FT of a Gaussian
is a Gaussian:

$$
X_m(k)=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\alpha_n^{(m)}\;
\underbrace{e^{-2\pi i\,k\cdot\mu_n}}_{\text{phase / shift}}\;
\underbrace{e^{-2\pi^2\,k^\top\Sigma_n k}}_{\text{magnitude / spread}} .
$$

Both paths share the same $\{\mu_n,\Sigma_n,\alpha_n^{(m)}\}$; only the discretization domain differs.

---

## 4. Alignment-aware contrast-private residual

The shared cloud is dominated by the high-SNR plain channel. Contrast-specific signal
(lesions, enhancement differences, residual misalignment) is absorbed by a set of private
Gaussians **rendered only into the contrast channel**
$\Phi=\{\mu_j^{\text{pv}},s_j^{\text{pv}},q_j^{\text{pv}},\beta_j\}_{j=1}^{N_{\text{pv}}}$
(in `_render("contrast")`: `vol = vol + pv`):

$$
\boxed{\,x_c(u)=\underbrace{\sum_{n}\alpha_n^{(c)}g_n(u)}_{\text{shared anatomical response}}
+\underbrace{\sum_{j}\beta_j\,g_j^{\text{pv}}(u)}_{\text{contrast-specific residual}}\,}
\qquad
x_p(u)=\sum_{n}\alpha_n^{(p)}g_n(u).
$$

The private set is supervised only by the contrast data-consistency term and constrained
to genuine contrast-specific deviations by the sparsity regularizer $\mathcal{R}_{\text{sp}}$ (§5).

---

## 5. Training objective (per-scan self-supervised, GT-free)

$$
\boxed{\;
\mathcal{L}=\underbrace{\mathcal{L}_{\text{dc}}^{p}+\mathcal{L}_{\text{dc}}^{c}}_{\text{dual k-space data consistency}}
+\lambda_{\text{geo}}\,\mathcal{R}_{\text{geo}}
+\lambda_{\text{dtv}}\,\mathcal{R}_{\text{dtv}}
+\lambda_{\text{sp}}\,\mathcal{R}_{\text{sp}}
\;}
$$

**(1) Dual data consistency** (`_kspace_loss`, weighted normalized k-space $\ell_2$):

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{dc}}^{m}=\frac{1}{\lVert M_m\rVert_1}
\Big\lVert\,W_m\odot M_m\odot\big(\mathcal{F}_{3c}(C_m\odot x_m)-y_m\big)\Big\rVert_2^2 .
$$

$W_m$: optional frequency-adaptive / curriculum weight (`_build_freq_weight` /
`_freq_weight_curriculum`; low weight at center, high at periphery, or coarse→fine). Both
terms backprop into the shared geometry — the mathematical origin of "dual-contrast joint
constraint on the geometry".

**(2) Geometric regularizer $\mathcal{R}_{\text{geo}}$** (`_tv_loss`, isotropic TV on magnitude):

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\text{geo}}=\mathrm{TV}(|x_p|)+\mathrm{TV}(|x_c|),\qquad
\mathrm{TV}(z)=\overline{|\partial_x z|}+\overline{|\partial_y z|}+\overline{|\partial_z z|}.
$$

Controlled by `loss.tv_weight` (read as $\lambda_{\text{geo}}$). **Primary anti-grain lever**
(sweet spot 0.05–0.06).

**(3) Plain-guided directional TV $\mathcal{R}_{\text{dtv}}$** (`_dtv_loss_directional`):

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\text{dtv}}=\sum_{d}\overline{\,w_d\odot|\partial_d |x_c|\,|},\qquad
w_d=w_{\text{floor}}+(1-w_{\text{floor}})\exp\!\Big(-\tfrac{|\partial_d|x_p|\,|}{\eta}\Big),
$$

with $|x_p|$ detached (guide only) and
$\eta=\eta_{\text{scale}}\cdot\tfrac13\sum_d\overline{|\partial_d|x_p||}+\epsilon$. Relaxes
the penalty at plain-channel strong edges (preserving contrast-specific structure), and
penalizes strongly where the plain channel is flat (suppressing aliasing/grain). Off by
default (`lambda_dtv=0`); a primary cross-contrast coupling knob for the ARC harness.

**(4) Private-residual sparsity $\mathcal{R}_{\text{sp}}$** ($\ell_1$, `lambda_private_sp`):

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\text{sp}}=\frac{1}{N_{\text{pv}}}\sum_{j}|\beta_j|.
$$

> Note (`INF-05 / A-LOSS-DROP`): the v1 hand-written cross-contrast priors
> $\mathcal{L}_{\text{diff}}, \mathcal{L}_{\text{enh}}$ are removed (config keys
> `lambda_diff`/`lambda_enh` are silently ignored); a Bayesian branch posterior
> $q(\alpha_{n,c})$ (the KL term of an ELBO) replaces them. This document is the
> deterministic main path.

**Optimization**: Adam with per-group learning rates (position/scale/rotation/density);
the shared geometry is warm-started first (`shared_warmup_iters`); the best checkpoint is
selected by **data-consistency loss** (not GT — a stricter criterion than GT-PSNR selection).

---

## 6. Adaptive capacity (densify, optional)

split/clone preserves **rendering-integral conservation** (render integral
$\propto|\alpha|\prod_i s_i$):

$$
\alpha_{\text{child}}=\frac{0.5}{\text{vol\_factor}}\,\alpha_{\text{parent}},\qquad
\text{vol\_factor}=\prod_i \frac{s_i^{\text{child}}}{s_i^{\text{parent}}}.
$$

so the two children's render integrals sum to the parent's. Under the shipped baseline
densify is **off** (warm-started from a ~577K-primitive cloud).

---

## 7. Inference: hard data-consistency evaluation head (`_hard_dc`)

$$
\hat{k}_m=\big(1-M_m\big)\odot \mathcal{F}_{3c}(C_m\odot x_m)+y_m,
\qquad
\hat{x}_m=\textstyle\sum_{\text{coil}}\bar{C}_m\odot \mathcal{F}_{3c}^{-1}(\hat{k}_m).
$$

Zero-filled reference $x_m^{\text{ZF}}=\sum_{\text{coil}}\bar C_m\odot\mathcal{F}_{3c}^{-1}(y_m)$
(`zf_p`/`zf_c`) serves as the normalization anchor.

---

## 8. One-page compression

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\textbf{Representation: }\ x_m=\textstyle\sum_n \alpha_n^{(m)}g_n(\cdot;\mu_n,\Sigma_n)\ (+\textstyle\sum_j\beta_j g_j^{\text{pv}}\ \text{only }m=c),\ \{\mu_n,\Sigma_n\}\ \textbf{shared}\\
&\textbf{Forward: }\ y_m=M_m\odot\mathcal{F}_{3c}(C_m\odot x_m)+\varepsilon_m\\
&\textbf{Objective: }\ \min \textstyle\sum_{m}\tfrac{1}{\|M_m\|_1}\|W_m M_m(\mathcal{F}_{3c}(C_m x_m)-y_m)\|_2^2
+\lambda_{\text{geo}}\mathcal{R}_{\text{geo}}+\lambda_{\text{dtv}}\mathcal{R}_{\text{dtv}}+\lambda_{\text{sp}}\mathcal{R}_{\text{sp}}\\
&\textbf{Inference: }\ \hat k_m=(1-M_m)\mathcal{F}_{3c}(C_m x_m)+y_m\ \to\ \hat x_m=\textstyle\sum_{\text{coil}}\bar C_m\mathcal{F}_{3c}^{-1}(\hat k_m)
\end{aligned}
$$

## 9. Why this is contrast-general

The model has **no T1/FLAIR-specific assumption** — $\{p,c\}$ are only "guide/target
contrast" placeholders. The directional-TV guide direction can be any high-SNR contrast
guiding any target. Any pair of co-registered, contrast-differing 3D acquisitions
(T1/T2, pre-/post-contrast, PD/T2, …) plugs directly into §2–§7. The only things bound to
a specific contrast pair are the hyperparameters
$(\lambda_{\text{geo}},\lambda_{\text{dtv}},\lambda_{\text{sp}},W_m)$ and the guide-direction
choice — these live in the **config layer, not the model layer**.
