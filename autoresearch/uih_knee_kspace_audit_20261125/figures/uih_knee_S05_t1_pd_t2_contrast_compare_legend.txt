T1, PD and T2 of subject S05 (UIH 3D knee set) at the same positions, and the ratio maps T1/PD and T2/PD. a, sagittal layer at readout index 90. b, section through the readout axis at n3 index 96 (coronal). Images: centred inverse 3D FFT, root-sum-of-squares over 12 coils, T1 and T2 moved onto PD by integer shifts [0, 0, 0] and [-1, -1, -1] voxels, each contrast divided by its median inside the knee mask; one grey scale [0, 3.41] for the three contrasts. Ratio maps: 3x3x3 box-smoothed images, drawn inside the mask, log2 colour scale from 0.35 to 2.8 centred at 1 (white); red = brighter than PD, blue = darker than PD.
