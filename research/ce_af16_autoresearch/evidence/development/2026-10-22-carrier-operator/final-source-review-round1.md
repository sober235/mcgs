4. **Accepted int32-safe volume sizes still overflow CUDA tile-dimension arithmetic.** `carrier_cuda/carrier_kernel.cu:20` computes `(H+7)/8` and `(W+7)/8` in signed int32. `carrier_cuda/carrier.cpp:22` and the Python validator allow individual dimensions up to INT32_MAX provided the voxel product fits int32.
